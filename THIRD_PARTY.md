## Color themes

The twenty color themes in `resources/color-themes` are named after popular themes of the Obsidian community (<https://community.obsidian.md/themes>), chosen by their downloads. Each file holds only a handful of colors that are written by hand to give the same impression, no style sheet and no other file of these projects is copied. The names and links are those of the original work.
